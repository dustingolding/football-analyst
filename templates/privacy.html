Privacy policy

Effective September 26, 2026. This policy covers the SidelineWire website (sidelinewire.com) and the SidelineWire iPhone app.

The short version

  • Accounts are optional. Without one, what we keep is tied to your device, not to you. We never ask for your location or contacts.
  • If you sign in with Apple, we keep your account so you can chat in private groups and keep your Beat the Model bankroll on all your devices. You can delete it in the app at any time.
  • We don't sell your data. The free app may show ads from Google AdMob; you choose whether Apple lets it use your device's advertising identifier, and SidelineWire Pro removes ads.

What the app sends us

When you allow notifications, the app registers your device with our server so we can send the alerts you choose. That registration contains:

  • A push notification token issued by Apple, and a random ID the app creates when it's installed.
  • The teams you follow and your alert settings, including any per-team and league-wide choices.
  • Your time zone, the app's bundle ID and whether it's a test or App Store build.
  • Live Activity tokens, if you follow a game on your Lock Screen, so we can update the score.

We also keep a list of the alerts we've sent to your device so the app can show your recent alerts.

Beat the Model (mock betting)

Beat the Model uses play units only; no real money is involved. If you play, we keep a random player ID the app creates, the nickname you choose, and your bets and results. Your nickname, record and profit appear on the public leaderboard.

Accounts (Sign in with Apple)

Signing in is optional and only needed for chat and for keeping your bankroll across devices. When you sign in with Apple we receive a user ID Apple creates for SidelineWire, and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address (which can be an Apple private relay address). We never see your Apple password. You can change the name other members see at any time.

Chat

Chat groups are private: only people with a group's invite code can join, and only members can read its messages. We store your messages, the groups you're in, and any messages you report or people you block. Messages you report, and who reported them, are kept so we can review them and act on abuse. Deleted messages are removed from the group.

What stays on your device

Your followed teams and settings are saved on your iPhone. The followed-teams list also syncs to your other devices through Apple's iCloud, under your Apple account; we never see your iCloud data. The app's widgets read the same list on your device.

Server logs and the website

Like most websites, our servers log requests (IP address, time, page requested and browser or app version) to keep the service running and secure. The website doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use it

Only to run SidelineWire: to decide which alerts to send, update lock-screen scores, show your alert history, and fix problems. We don't use your SidelineWire data for advertising or profiling; ads are served by Google AdMob as described above.

Ads

The free app can show ads served by Google AdMob, including optional videos you can watch for Beat the Model bonus units. AdMob collects device and usage information to serve and measure ads. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, ads can be personalized using your device's advertising identifier; if you don't, you'll still see ads, just not personalized ones. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security › Tracking. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

For a bonus-unit video, Google tells our server which Beat the Model player watched it so we can add the units; that's the only ad information we receive.

SidelineWire Pro

Pro is an optional subscription bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks your subscription with Apple on your device to turn off ads.

Who else is involved

  • Apple delivers notifications and Live Activities, syncs your followed teams through iCloud, and handles Pro subscriptions.
  • Google AdMob serves the ads in the free app.
  • Team logos are loaded from ESPN's image servers, which receive your IP address like any website you load an image from.

We don't sell or share your data with anyone else. We run our own servers.

How long we keep it

Your account, bets and chat messages are kept until you delete them or your account. Deleting your account (in the app: Alerts → Account → Delete Account) permanently removes your account, your Beat the Model bankroll and bets, your messages and the groups you created, and ends Sign in with Apple for SidelineWire.

Device registrations are kept while the app keeps using them. When Apple reports that a device can no longer receive notifications (for example, the app was deleted), we stop sending and mark it inactive. Alert history and server logs are kept only as long as needed to run the service.

Your choices

You can delete your account and its data at any time in the app under Alerts → Account. You can turn any alert off in the app, turn off notifications in iOS Settings, or delete the app. To have your device's data deleted from our server, {% if support_email %}email {{ support_email }}{% else %}contact us through our support page{% endif %}. Deleting the app and turning off notifications also stops all alerts, and we then mark the device inactive.

Children

SidelineWire isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact
