Common questions

How do I follow a team?

Tap the star on any team page, use + in the Alerts tab, or pick teams when you first open the app. Your teams' games move to the front of Home and Scores.

I'm not getting alerts

Check that notifications are on for SidelineWire in iOS Settings → Notifications, then check the switches in the app's Alerts tab. Each team can have its own settings under Per-Team Settings. Alerts are sent for games with live data, so there's nothing to send between games.

How do I join a friend's chat group?

Sign in with Apple under Alerts → Account, then open Chat and tap Join a Group and enter the 8-character invite code your friend shared (or open their invite link on your iPhone). Group creators can make a new code at any time, which stops the old one from working.

Someone is harassing me in a group

Touch and hold their message to report it or block them. Blocked members' messages are hidden from you and don't alert you. You can also leave the group. We review reported messages{% if support_email %}; to reach us directly, email {{ support_email }}{% endif %}.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Alerts → Account → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, your Beat the Model bankroll and bets, your messages and the groups you created.

How do the lock-screen scores work?

On a live game's page, tap Follow on Lock Screen. With Auto-follow on Lock Screen turned on in Alerts, your teams' games appear there automatically when they kick off. Live Activities must be allowed for SidelineWire in iOS Settings.

Where do the predictions come from?

Our own models, built from box scores and play-by-play and tested on seasons they never saw. How our models work explains them.

Can I bet in the app?

No. SidelineWire shows predictions and betting lines for information and entertainment. It doesn't take wagers or link to sportsbooks.

How do I delete my data?

SidelineWire has no accounts. Turning off notifications or deleting the app stops alerts, and the server forgets devices it can no longer reach. To have your data removed right away, contact us. See our privacy policy for details.
