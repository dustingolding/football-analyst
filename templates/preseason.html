Elite is the chance of a top-10 season. Studying every FBS season from 2016 to 2025, elite seasons came from sustained program strength, a roster built from 4- and 5-star recruits (elite teams average 51% blue-chips versus 11% for everyone else) and coaching stability; transfer-portal volume and returning production didn't help predict them.
